How do I scan a clock?

Open ClockBro, point the camera at an analog clock and fill the circle with the clock face. Hold still for a moment: the ring fills and the time appears. For a clock that hangs high or far away, zoom in with a pinch or the zoom buttons.

The reading is wrong. What can I do?

  • Move closer or zoom in so the clock fills the circle.
  • Hold the phone straight in front of the clock face, not at a steep angle.
  • Avoid strong glare and very dark light.

The small clock icon next to the result shows the hands the app read, so you can compare it with the real clock.

Why does the free version need the internet?

The free version is funded by ads, so it works only online. ClockBro PRO removes ads and works fully offline. Reading the clock itself always happens on your phone.

How do I restore ClockBro PRO?

Open Settings (gear icon) → Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought PRO with. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, German, Russian and Polish. ClockBro follows your phone's language; you can change it in Settings → Language.

Is my camera data private?

Yes. Recognition runs on your device and camera images never leave it. Details: Privacy Policy.
