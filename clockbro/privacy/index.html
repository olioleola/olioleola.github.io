ClockBro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

In short: ClockBro reads the clock on your phone. Camera images and photos never leave your device. There are no accounts. We do not collect your name, email or contacts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; purchases go through Apple; crash reports go to Sentry without your IP address.

Who is responsible

ClockBro is made by Aleh Siarheyeu, a sole trader in Poland ("we", "us"). Contact: olioleolapps@gmail.com.

Camera and photos

The clock is recognised by a model that runs entirely on your device. Camera frames and photos you pick from your gallery are processed in memory to read the time and are not stored, uploaded or shared. We never receive them.

Data processed by third-party services

ClockBro has no server of its own. The services below receive data directly from the app.

Google AdMob (free version only)

The free version shows ads (a banner and occasional full-screen videos). Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect: device identifiers (an identifier limited to apps of one developer; ClockBro does not ask for permission to track, so the iOS advertising identifier is not available to it), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video views), app performance and crash data related to ads. Google uses this data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. In the EEA and the UK you can change or withdraw your ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use data only for limited purposes such as frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.

More: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

ClockBro PRO shows no ads, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK does not request ads for PRO users.

Apple and RevenueCat (purchases)

ClockBro PRO is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. To check whether you own PRO, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history for ClockBro (product, date, store receipt), the device's country and currency, and the app version. More: RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy.

Sentry (crash reports)

If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), stored in Sentry's EU data region. It contains technical data: the error and code location, app version, device model, operating system version and similar diagnostics. We have turned off IP address storage, and the app does not send names, emails or other personal content. Crash reports are deleted automatically after the retention period of our Sentry plan (at most 90 days). More: Sentry Privacy Policy.

App updates and connectivity checks

The app checks for small updates on Expo's update service (expo.dev), sending the app version and platform. The free version checks whether your device is online by contacting a Google server. As with any internet request, these servers see your IP address. More: Expo Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a few settings on your device only: whether you have seen the introduction, your language choice, a local copy of your PRO status, and counters that space out full-screen ads. Deleting the app removes them.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Non-personalised ads, crash reports, update and connectivity checks: our legitimate interest in running, financing and fixing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchases and restoring them: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Tracking

ClockBro does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, does not ask for permission to track, and does not sell your personal data.

Children

ClockBro is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account data and receive no camera images, most data sits with the services above; write to us and we will help you reach them. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl) or the authority in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
